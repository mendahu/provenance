Rules the dialog encodes

Unknown is not zero

A missing part is missing. A day with no hour is a day, not midnight. Finer fields stay disabled until every coarser one on that side is set, so a gap cannot be entered at all. Precision is what you filled in — there is no separate “year only” mode to choose — precision is inferred from the parts present, never declared.

Qualifier is for points

As stated, ABT, BEF, AFT apply to a single date at any precision. Choosing Between hides the control and clears the value — the window is the approximation, so a second hedge on top of it would say nothing.

Time is secondary

Clock time appears only once a full calendar day exists, and only behind “Add time”. Most genealogical dates never open it. Zones are free text, never a forced picker.

Errors are calm

Validation is inline, under the group that owns it, and the confirm button stays disabled until the form is valid for the chosen kind. No toasts for ordinary incompleteness.
